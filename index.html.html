Why is tan undefined at 90° and 270°?

At those angles the point is (0, ±1), so cos θ = 0. Since tan θ = sin θ / cos θ (the slope of the radius), you would divide by zero: the radius is vertical and has no finite slope. Likewise csc and cot are undefined at 0° and 180° where sin θ = 0.

45°–45°–90°

Isosceles right triangle: equal legs of length 1 give hypotenuse √2.

sin 45° = √2/2 cos 45° = √2/2 tan 45° = 1
30°–60°–90°

Half-equilateral triangle of side 2 with legs 1 and √3.

sin 30° = 1/2 cos 30° = √3/2 tan 30° = √3/3
